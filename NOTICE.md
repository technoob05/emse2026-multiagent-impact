# Scope of the licence

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the analysis code, figure code, build
scripts, and the derived analysis products in this repository.
It does NOT cover:

- The AIDev-7.6M release (`hao-li/AIDev-7.6M`), which is third-party data under
  its own terms. This repository pins a revision and reads it; it does not
  redistribute it.
- Any other third-party dataset screened during the external-evidence audit.
  Those acquisitions are recorded by revision, licence, and checksum in
  `docs/audits/EXTERNAL_DATA_PROVENANCE_20260826.md` and are deliberately not
  committed here.
- The manuscript text and figures under `paper/`, whose rights transfer is
  governed by the publisher agreement once the paper is accepted.

The repository analyses public GitHub metadata about real accounts. Anyone
reusing it should respect GitHub's terms and applicable data-protection law.
