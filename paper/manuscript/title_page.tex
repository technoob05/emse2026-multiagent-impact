\textbf{\large Statements and Declarations}

\textbf{Funding.} The authors received no funding for this work.

\textbf{Competing interests.} The authors declare no competing interests. None
of the authors is affiliated with, employed by, or funded by any of the
coding-agent vendors whose products this study measures.

\textbf{Ethics approval and consent to participate.} Ethics approval was not
required. This study analyses pull-request records that GitHub already
publishes, collected through a public research dataset, and involves no
intervention and no interaction with any person. No participants were recruited,
so consent to participate does not apply. We report accounts by role rather than
by login throughout, and no figure or table in this article or in Online
Resource~1 names an individual.

\textbf{Consent for publication.} Not applicable. No individual-level
identifying information is reproduced in this article. The analyses use public
GitHub repository records, and accounts are reported by role and in aggregate
rather than by login.

\textbf{Data availability.} The source data are the public AIDev-7.6M release at
the pinned revision reported in the article's method section. The derived
cohorts needed to reproduce every result are archived at
\url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22140821}.

\textbf{Materials availability.} Not applicable. This study uses no physical
materials.

\textbf{Code availability.} The analysis and figure code that produces every
table and figure in this article and in Online Resource~1 is archived with the
derived cohorts at
\url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22140821}
and is also available at
\url{https://github.com/technoob05/emse2026-multiagent-impact}.
